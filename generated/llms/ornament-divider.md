## OrnamentDivider (`ew-ornament-divider`)

Structural divider; eras supply ornament via decorators.

HTML:
```html
<hr class="ew-ornament-divider" data-variant="ornament" />
```

CSS: @era-widgets/css/ornament-divider.css + @era-widgets/css/eras/<era>.css
Variants: variant=line|ornament(default:line)
Sizes: sm|md|lg (default md)
ARIA: separator
Do: Use for sectioning, not for layout hacks.
Don't: Don't wallpaper every section with costume ornaments at low fidelity.
