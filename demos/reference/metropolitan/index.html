On the program

Harbor Lights

A tone poem in three movements. The score follows a night boat from the battery to the narrows, and the lighting plot does the same: a line of lamps, then open water.

Speed

A one-act for six voices and a filmed horizon. No scenery beyond a chrome rail and the cyclorama. Fifty-five minutes, without an interval.

The house

Arrival

The doors on 40th Street open one hour before curtain. Cloakroom to the left, bronze stairs to the mezzanine, elevator to the balcony.

Late seating is at a suitable pause. The lobby bar pours until the last call of the second intermission.
