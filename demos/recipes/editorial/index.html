Salt on the sill

IV

I. Voss · Fictional essay

The boarding house kept a china bowl of salt on every sill, not for luck but for the damp. By October the crystals crusted and the panes drew small maps of the harbor.

A place becomes memorable when its maintenance is visible. The bowl, the stair rod, the ledger taped at the corner: these are the sentences.
