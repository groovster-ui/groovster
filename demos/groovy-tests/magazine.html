Issue no. 12

The long afternoon

A fictional essay on paper lanterns, porch radios, and the color of late sun.

AL

Ada Lovelace · Field notes

The porch radio kept the afternoon in one key. Someone had taped the dial so it could not leave the station that played only instrumentals, and the speaker cloth had faded to the same yellow as the awning.

We measured the day by when the shadow of the citrus tree reached the steps. Before that line, the magazine stayed open. After it, the pages went warm and the type looked printed in honey.

Nothing in this issue is a real assignment. The departments that follow are sample stories, set so the poster skin can carry a reading page without climbing into the paragraph.
