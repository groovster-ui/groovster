Issue 7 · The line

A stem that draws the page

How a single tapering stroke holds a poster, a room, and a sentence.

CM C. Moreau · a fictional correspondent

The lithograph keeps its colors apart. Gold is a ring, olive is a stem, madder is the bloom behind the line. None of them is a texture laid over the words.

A Mucha sheet reserves a panel for the name. The ornament grows around that silence. When the same idea is asked to be a website, the panel is the paragraph and the button, and the halo stays to one side.

This essay is sample copy for the demonstration. It is not a historical paper.
