Issue 07 · The sheet

element

A quarterly on circles, squares, and the work of putting them in order.

The page is a sheet of paper. A spine divides the words from the construction, and every form has to share an edge with that spine or with the rule at the top.

This issue follows three workshops: color, type, and the object that has to survive both.

Departments

Red is the square

A plane, not a sticker. It takes the top of the field.

Blue is the circle

Cropped by the sheet so only the part that matters remains.

Yellow is the triangle

Its right angle sits on the spine and the bottom rule.

Display words stay lowercase. Labels are tracked capitals. Nothing functional is turned on its side.

A lamp, a stool, a poster. Each one is a plane with a job.
