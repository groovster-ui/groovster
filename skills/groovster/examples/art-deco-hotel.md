# Example: elegant art deco hotel

User: "Build an elegant art deco hotel booking website."

1. Recipe: `hospitality`.
2. Era: `deco`. Fidelity: `costume` on the arrival and rooms.
3. Sections: masthead, rooms, reserve, visit.
4. Hero: `.ew-hero__panel` with rays and fan, and `.ew-hero__stage` so other eras still have hooks.
5. Reservation uses native dates, a guest select, room radios, and a textarea. The confirmation says no room is held.
6. No React. No payment provider.
